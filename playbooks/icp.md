# Ideal Customer Profile (Draft)

## Offer

Fiber Scout is researching prospects for a Florida business-fiber provider
offering 1 Gbps, 5 Gbps, and 10 Gbps services, plus static IP addresses.
Pricing, service-level commitments, and other technical details have not
been provided and must not be assumed.

## Geography and eligibility

- The prospecting list contains Florida addresses only.
- The business owner has confirmed that addresses supplied for research are
  serviceable. Treat that as user-provided input, not as an independently
  verified claim by the agent.
- Research each address provided. Do not change or replace its input data.
- If a business operates at multiple supplied addresses, treat each address
  as a separate prospect/account.

## Target segments

All of the following segments are in scope and have equal priority:

- Commercial businesses
- Commercial multi-tenant units (MTUs), including their property or building
  managers where relevant
- Government buildings and municipalities
- Industrial businesses

Churches are a known, relatively easy-to-reach prospect type based on the
owner's experience. National chain corporations, such as McDonald's or
Chipotle, are known to be harder to sell to. Use these observations to guide
research into decision-making and reachability; do not automatically exclude
or rank an organization based on its name or industry.

## Potential fit signals

Look for evidence—not assumptions—of:

- High bandwidth demand
- Multiple locations (each address requires its own account)
- Dependence on cloud services, voice over IP (VoIP), video, security, or
  other connected systems
- A potential use for static IP addresses
- A reachable local owner or decision-maker
- A building or property manager who can approve service for MTU tenants

These are research signals for the later scoring step, not claims to make
about a business without supporting evidence.

## Likely decision-makers to research

Roles are leads for research, not guaranteed contacts:

- Churches: pastor; owner or administrator if applicable
- Other single-site businesses: owner, manager, or IT decision-maker
- MTUs: property/building manager or landlord for building approval; a
  tenant's decision-maker for its own account
- Government and municipalities: IT, procurement, network, or relevant
  municipal decision-maker
- Industrial businesses and larger organizations: IT, operations,
  procurement, or an owner/manager with buying authority

Identify all businesses found at a multi-tenant address. Do not guess which
tenant is intended.

## Qualification principles

- Research all target segments; none has a predetermined scoring advantage.
- Base fit judgments on sourced evidence and the scoring rubric.
- Do not infer bandwidth requirements, static-IP needs, buying authority, or
  willingness to purchase from industry alone.
- Treat unknown information as unknown, not as a negative fact.
